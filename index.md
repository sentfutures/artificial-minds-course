# Artificial Minds Research Course

*Sentient Futures · Version 4.4 · Published for review*

An eight-week online course, plus a two-week judging window, that trains newcomers to do rigorous work on artificial minds. Each week is a 90-minute live session, required reading, and homework, ending in a final project proposal reviewed against five published criteria.

## Course documents

- [Syllabus](01-syllabus.md) — readings, requirements, deadlines and brief session agendas
- [Course Companion](02-course-companion.md) — teaching, proposal template, worked examples, homework handouts and the optional archive
- [Glossary](03-glossary.md) — weekly terms plus optional methods reference
- [Facilitator Guide](04-facilitator-guide.md) — running each session and reviewing homework
- [Facilitator Appendices](05-facilitator-appendices.md) — keys, rubric, templates and course-channel setup
- [Concept Atlas outline](09-concept-atlas.md) — the course concepts organised by topic, with week and assessment mappings

## Interactive tools

- [Artificial Minds Map](atlas/) — the Concept Atlas, drawn: click a branch to open it, search terms, read definitions in place
- [Forecast Yourself](forecast-yourself.html) — consider the survey arguments, record your own probability, compare it with the expert median

The map and the outline are generated from the same [atlas.json](atlas.json); the outline carries every term and definition as text and is the accessible version.
